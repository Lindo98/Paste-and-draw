Add it to Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings → See all settings → General → Signature.
  3. Create a new signature (or edit yours), click in the box and paste with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac).
  4. Scroll down and click Save Changes.

Use Chrome or Edge for copying. If images show as broken right after pasting, give it a moment — Gmail loads them from the links.
